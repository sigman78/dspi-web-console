# Noveau review — fit-to-viewport lens (2026-10-25)

## 1. Verdict on the recommendation

C-as-skeleton is half right. Keep its incremental path (TUNE first, behind `?ui=noveau`), its inspector row language, and its type scale. Its layout does not meet the no-scroll rule, and its own screenshots show this. `mock-c-tune.png` has a page scrollbar on both axes. The inspector stops at "Gain reduction", with Processors still below it, and that is at 1600×1000, a viewport no maximised browser gives you. `mock-c-listen.png` also scrolls ("Recent" is cut off) on the emptiest screen of the set. SETUP is specified as "one scrolling page", which breaks the constraint by design.

The binding axis is height, not width. A 1440×900 MacBook gives about 1440×790 of viewport. A 1920×1080 Windows laptop at 125 % scaling gives about 1536×740. **Design to 1400×740 as the floor.** 1600×1000 is not a reference; it is a best case.

What I would build instead:

- **Shell:** a 56 px icon rail (from A) replaces C's 200 px sidebar, which spends a full-height column on four links.
- **Chrome:** a persistent top bar holds the scene bank, quick toggles, volume, mute, bypass, and a health dot (from B and C's LISTEN).
- **Channel rail:** today's left rail stays, with meters, as the selector and meter bridge.
- **Three destinations:** TUNE, PROGRAM and SETUP. LIBRARY becomes a sheet.

LISTEN as a destination fails: the daily controls must stay reachable while you tune, and once they live in the chrome, the LISTEN page has nothing left.

## 2. Direction-by-direction critique

**A — Signal Path**

- **The drawer is too small for the core task.** At 260 px it holds crossfeed, which is the demo's easy case. It cannot hold a 10-band EQ graph plus table, upmix (13 rows), or limiter link groups. At 740 the canvas gets 440 px for 9 lanes, so a lane is about 48 px. Either the drawer or the flow is unusable, never both.
- **"Tune one output" takes four clicks** (XOVER·EQ → TRIM → DELAY → LIMITER). Each click swaps the drawer, so the parameters you need together are never on screen together. This recreates pain point 1 inside a single page.
- **The layout is hard-coded for 1600 px.** Nodes use absolute coordinates. The "layout engine" risk is understated: nothing here reflows to 1400 px or to RP2040's 5 outputs.
- **Floating layers cover the data.** The pending dock floats over lanes 7–9, the health popover covers output nodes, and Psybass has no block at all.

**B — Channel Strips**

- **It does not fit at 1400 px wide.** 8 inputs × 64 + 9 outputs × 100 + 250 for PROGRAM is about 1660 px. It only fits if inputs collapse to stereo pairs and outputs drop to about 88 px, and then the values truncate ("-6.0 dB ⊙").
- **The strip is summary-only.** Its focus pane collapses the strips into a meter bridge and opens a full editor, which is C's TUNE. So B really becomes C with a different home screen.
- **The SOURCE block grows with routing.** An output fed by all 8 inputs needs 8 rows, which squeezes the meter at 740. Vertically this is the only elastic element, so it degrades gracefully, but the full matrix has no home.
- **The PROGRAM column has the same scrolling problem as C's inspector.** With leveller, upmix and subharm all on, it is about 1100 px of parameters in a 900 px column. Tall faders spend about 150 px each for mouse-precision trim that a drag field does in 24 px. SETUP is "a separate page" with no design.

**C — Focus + Dock**

- **The inspector stacks five sections in one column.** Band, sources, output, limiter and membership come to about 1050 px against about 680 px available. Sources already hides behind "6 more…", so the matrix column is truncated even before the inspector scrolls.
- **The horizontal channel chips do not scale.** 17 chips in about 1000 px means 55 px each at 1400, so names truncate ("Out 3 Le…"). A vertical rail holds 17 rows of 30 px in 510 px.
- **Program processors are homeless.** Toggles sit on LISTEN, parameters in the "TUNE-input inspector", yet crossfeed, upmix and leveller are stereo or global, not per input. Psybass parameters and the V32 tube preamp appear nowhere, in any direction.
- **SETUP and the control surfaces are where scrolling actually lives today**, and C defers them to a scrolling page. That is the hardest fit problem, and no direction solves it.

## 3. Fit-to-viewport plan

**Shell**

- `html, body { overflow: hidden }`. The app is a grid of `100dvh × 100dvw` with `min-width: 1400px; min-height: 740px`.
- Fixed rows: top bar 44 px and status/commit line 28 px. The workspace gets 668 px at the floor.
- The pending-changes dock lives in the status line and expands as an upward popover. It never floats over content.

**Elastic vs fixed**

- Only the **EQ graph, the meters, and the chain canvas** absorb leftover space.
- Everything else has an intrinsic height, sized for the worst case: 8 in / 9 out, all gates open.

**TUNE at 668 px**

| Area | Content | Height |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graph | Editable EQ graph | ~430 px, elastic |
| Under the graph | Output-stage band in two rows: trim · delay · phase · mute · limiter thr/rel/GR · link, plus membership chips LEV·LOUD·PSY·SUB | 72 px |
| Under the graph | Toolbar | 32 px |
| Right inspector (300 px wide) | Selected band | 120 px |
| Right inspector | 10-row band table (keep it: the graph alone is too imprecise) | 10 × 24 = 260 px |
| Right inspector | Sources: the full matrix column, 8 × 24 | 220 px |

The inspector totals about 600 px, inside the 668 px budget.

**PROGRAM (new destination)**

- A's program blocks, laid out horizontally as a chain strip: 72 px, each showing on/off and two key values. Include psybass and tube.
- Full matrix on the left: 8 × 9 cells at 32 px, about 340 × 320. Cell gain and phase are edited in a popover.
- Detail of the selected module on the right, in a fixed area. Upmix is the worst case at 13 × 24 = 312 px.
- Limiter link groups also go here; per-output GR shows on the rail meters.

**SETUP**

- Paged with a segmented control: Device · Clocks & I/O · Pins · Interfaces · Surfaces · Display · Diagnostics.
- Each page is designed against the 668 px budget, with one commit idiom (the status-line dock).
- Surfaces, macros and display pages use master–detail sized to the firmware's fixed slot maximum.

**Density rules**

| Element | Size |
| --- | --- |
| Inspector rows | 24 px |
| Section headers | 28 px |
| Labels | 11 px sans minimum |
| Values | 12 px mono |
| Axis ticks only | 10 px |

Nothing at 9 px. Gutters are 12 / 16 px.

**Overlays**

These are allowed: AutoEQ, the matrix cell popover, the health popover, the LIBRARY sheet, and the command palette.

**Enforcement**

Add a Playwright check at 1400×740 and 1600×880 with `?mock=multi` for every destination. It asserts:

- `scrollHeight <= innerHeight` on the document;
- no element has `scrollHeight > clientHeight`, except a whitelist: event log, changelog, AutoEQ results.

**Below the floor**

- Use a single page-level pan on the 1400×740 canvas, as today. No reflow: reflow doubles the layout test matrix for a niche case.
- Below about 1000 px wide, show the existing splash.
- Do not build a "compact mode".

## 4. What to steal and what to cut

**Steal**

- **From B:**
  - The scene bank as permanent chrome.
  - The membership toggles per output (they replace the five mask rows).
  - Disabled outputs collapsing to a 24 px vertical stub ("Out 3 Left · HW OFF").
  - The persistent status line (fw · wire · rate · link · "nothing staged").
- **From A:**
  - The 56 px icon rail.
  - The PASS-order program chain as the spine of PROGRAM.
  - The compact matrix node as an always-visible map.
  - The three-state legend (lit / dim / hatched).
  - Clip badges on the node that owns them.
- **From C:**
  - The inspector rows and type scale.
  - The draggable graph with the value callout.
  - The "Link with Out 1 Right" toggle (issue #138).
  - The health tiles that stay dark when nominal, moved into the popover.
  - Ctrl+K.

**Cut**

- The LISTEN destination. The 96 px volume readout is decoration.
- C's 200 px nav sidebar.
- The horizontal channel chips.
- The "6 more…" sources truncation.
- B's tall faders.
- A's canvas as a primary editor (keep it, at most, as a read-only overview later).
- "Recent" activity.
- SETUP as one scrolling page.
- The claim that the matrix "is one click away" as an overlay: at 8×9 it fits permanently on PROGRAM.

## 5. Open questions for the owner

1. **Floor:** is a 125 %-scaled Windows laptop (about 1536×740) a must-fit, or is the floor 1440×900 logical (about 1440×790)? The answer moves the vertical budget by about 50 px and decides whether the band table and the sources can share the inspector.
2. **Stereo pair as the default unit:** should the rail and TUNE treat 1L/1R as one channel with an L/R sub-selector? This halves the channel lists and the matrix, and interacts with the #138 decision (default unlocked).
3. **Matrix thinking:** do users think in the matrix (a permanent panel on PROGRAM) or only in per-output sources (TUNE)? This decides whether PROGRAM and routing are one page.
4. **SETUP:** are the control-surface, macro and display-page counts bounded by firmware slot maximums? If yes, every page can be sized to its maximum. If no, may SETUP › Surfaces be the single whitelisted scroller?
5. **Daily controls in the chrome:** do you accept a top bar about 44 px tall carrying scenes, toggles and volume on every screen, at the cost of graph height? Or should listening stay a separate place?
